# Content production plan: 2,000+ JEE questions and 92+ quizzes

Do not fill a question bank with unverified or copied material. Build a traceable, reviewed collection.

Suggested initial distribution (editable):
- Physics: 700 questions
- Chemistry: 650 questions
- Mathematics: 700 questions
- Total: 2,050 questions

Suggested quiz collection:
- 30 Physics chapter/topic quizzes
- 31 Chemistry chapter/topic quizzes
- 31 Mathematics chapter/topic quizzes
- Total: 92 quizzes

For each question store:
- Subject, chapter, topic, exam pattern and question type
- Difficulty (foundation / moderate / advanced)
- Prompt and optional diagram/image
- Options or numerical answer
- Correct answer and step-by-step explanation
- Source/licensing status and reviewer
- Marking scheme and tags

For every chapter create:
1. One-page short notes (definitions, formulas, exceptions).
2. Formula/reaction sheet where relevant.
3. A mind map with clear relationships.
4. Original diagrams or properly licensed images with alt text.
5. 2–4 topic quizzes and a mixed chapter test.

Use current official syllabi and marking schemes as the reference. Review numerical answers, units, diagrams, and explanations with a qualified subject teacher before publishing.
